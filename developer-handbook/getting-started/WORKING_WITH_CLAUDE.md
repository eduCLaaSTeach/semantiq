# Working With Claude

A short mindset note for the team. Claude can generate code quickly, but the quality of what you get back depends on the quality of your thinking. Lead with design and logic; let Claude handle the typing.

## Think And Design First

- Every developer must think and design before asking Claude to build. Decide what the solution should do, how the pieces fit, and what "correct" looks like before any code is written.
- The clearer your design and reasoning, the more workable the final outcome. A well-thought design produces a solution that actually fits the problem; a vague request produces plausible-looking code that misses it.

## Design And Logic Over Coding

- Design and logical-reasoning skills matter more than raw coding skill when you work with Claude. Your job shifts from writing every line to defining the problem, the constraints, the data flow, and the edge cases.
- Code is most useful to you as a debugging and verification aid: read it to check that the logic matches your design, to trace a bug, and to confirm behavior, not as the main thing you produce by hand.

## Keep Improving

- Invest in your design and logical thinking. Those are the skills that make Claude productive for you, and that separate a workable solution from one that merely compiles.
