# Decision / Conflict Register

The project's dated decision log and its open questions, beside every conflict and gap that needs a human decision. Type names what the row is: a decision, an open question, or a finding carrying its classification label (`CONFLICT`, `GAP`, `STANDARD CONFLICT`, `ASSUMPTION REQUIRED`).

Never silently reverse a recorded decision. A reversal is a new dated row that names the decision it replaces.
